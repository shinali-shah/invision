            How this works

            Cues come from a pretrained face model (MediaPipe Face Landmarker) running in this browser. It measures smiling, facing and nodding, not feelings or sincerity.

            Numbers are computed without AI; Muse Spark only writes the insight, and every claim points to a moment you can play.

            The video stays on this Mac. Face analysis runs locally in Chrome at 5 frames per second of video; only the per-frame numbers are sent to the local server.
